Source-linked cards

Every card linked to its source.

Cards are made with the page and line they came from. When you're stuck, the PDF opens with that line highlighted.

  • Review cards before they join your deck
  • Select a line in the PDF and tap "Write a card from this line"
  • Scanned pages are read on your device
  • Export your cards to Anki (Pro)

Smart review

Review before you forget.

Reviews are scheduled by how well you remember (FSRS). Add your exam date and no card is left for after the exam.

  • Again, Hard, Good, Easy: the next review is planned for you
  • Your daily goal adjusts to your exam date
  • Keep it steady with your streak flame and daily reminders
  • Translate a card, get an explanation or ask for more

Quizzes and summaries

Test yourself, read the summary.

Multiple-choice questions that test the topic, and summaries section by section. Every question and section is linked to its page in the PDF.

  • Easy, medium and hard questions
  • Tap "Why?" to see the explanation
  • Summaries split into sections, with source pages
  • Choose the pages, language and question types

Mind maps

See the topic at a glance.

Main topics and concepts come together in a living map. With Pro, a new PDF's map is prepared for you.

  • Pan, zoom and drag the nodes
  • Tap a node for a one-line note and its line in the PDF
  • Topics and concepts linked together
  • Replay the map with the magic wand

Ask Tabi

Stuck? Ask Tabi.

Ask about anything you don't get, on a card, in a quiz or right in the PDF. Tabi starts from your document and shows the page the answer came from; when a slide only gives a heading, it explains the topic.

  • Select a paragraph in the PDF and tap "Ask Tabi"
  • Give an example, a memory tip, or simplify
  • Tells you when it explains beyond the slide
  • 5 questions a day free, 30 with Pro

Frequently asked questions

Is FormlyStudy free?

It's free to download and use. Free use renews 30 pages of AI every month, with 3 documents and 5 Tabi questions a day. FormlyStudy Pro unlocks 500 pages a month, 50 documents and 30 Tabi questions a day, iCloud sync and Anki export. Pro is a weekly, monthly or yearly subscription; the yearly plan includes a 3-day free trial.

Are my documents sent to a server?

Your PDF file isn't. Cards, quizzes, summaries, mind maps and Tabi are prepared with AI in the cloud; only the text of the pages you choose is sent, after you agree the first time. You can withdraw consent anytime in Settings › Preferences › Cloud consent.

Will my cards show up on my other device?

With Pro, turn on iCloud sync and your library moves between the iPhone and iPad signed in to the same Apple Account. By default, PDFs sync only over Wi-Fi.

Which devices does it run on?

Every iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later. The AI runs in the cloud, so Apple Intelligence isn't needed, just an internet connection. Reviewing your cards works offline.

How do I manage my subscription?

In iPhone Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions, or Settings › Manage subscription in the app. If your Pro purchase doesn't show up, tap "Restore purchases" on the Pro screen with the same Apple Account.

Who do I contact if something goes wrong?

Write to the address on the support page with your app version and a short description of the problem.

Tabi keeps your place.

The orange ribbon bookmark that keeps your place in the book. It waits while your cards are being made, cheers when you keep your streak, and reminds you of the cards you forget.
