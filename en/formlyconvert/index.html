Frequently asked questions

Is FormlyConvert free?

You can download and use the app for free. Formly Pro gives you unlimited conversions without ads, with monthly, yearly and lifetime options. Subscriptions are managed in your App Store account settings.

Are my files uploaded to the internet?

No. Converting, compressing and editing happen on your device; file content isn't uploaded to our servers. If you turn on iCloud sync, your library is stored in your own iCloud account.

How do I open a UDF file?

Share the UDF file to FormlyConvert from Files, Mail or WhatsApp and choose the UDF to PDF tool. Save or send the result as a PDF.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. On iPad you can take notes with Apple Pencil.

Who do I contact if something goes wrong?

Write to the address on the support page with your app version and a short description of the problem.
