FormlyConvert Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains how FormlyConvert ("the app") handles your documents and other data, and what choices you have. The data controller is Süleyman Çayır; contact: destek@formlyapps.com.

In short

  • You don't need an account; we don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Converting, compressing, OCR, signing and filling in forms happen on your device. Your files are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Data goes to Apple only in two features you turn on yourself: Apple Private Cloud Compute in the Document Assistant, and speech recognition you approve for a single job.
  • The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads are personalized only with your permission.
  • We don't sell your data.

1. Your files and on-device processing

Converting, compressing, merging, extracting text from PDFs (with OCR where needed), reading QR codes, signing, stamping and filling in forms all take place on your device. Imported files, results and Library records (name, tool, date, folder, pin) are stored in the app's area on your device. Files you pick from iCloud Drive or another storage provider may also be synced by that provider. You control the copies you export or share.

If you use "Update the original in Files", the result replaces the original file in Files only after you confirm.

2. Form profile

The details you enter for "Fill with my details", such as your name, address or phone number, are stored only on your device, in the app's own area, and are used to fill in form fields. They are not sent to us or to third parties. You can edit or delete the profile in the app at any time.

3. Optional iCloud Library sync

Library sync is off by default and available with Pro. If you turn it on, the files in your Library and a small list of details (names, tools, dates, folders, pins) are copied to the app's folder in iCloud Drive ("Formly"), so your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account show the same Library. This data is stored in your own iCloud account under Apple's privacy practices and does not pass through our servers. Deleting an item while sync is on also removes it from your other devices. Turning sync off stops new copies; files already in iCloud Drive stay until you delete them.

4. Share extension, widgets, Spotlight and Shortcuts

Files you share to FormlyConvert from another app are copied to an area on your device that only the app and its extensions can use, and are moved into the app when it opens; shares you cancel are removed. Widgets show the names, types and small previews of your recent Library files. Library file names are indexed on your device so you can find them in Spotlight; you can turn this off in Settings. Shortcuts and Live Activities also run on your device. Nothing is uploaded for these features.

5. Document Assistant and Apple Private Cloud Compute

The Document Assistant extracts the readable text of the PDF you choose on your device. Apple Private Cloud Compute (PCC) is off by default. If you turn on "Use Apple PCC for document analysis", the text needed for a summary or answer, together with your question where applicable, is sent to Apple's PCC service. The original PDF file is not sent. Long documents may need several requests. The text you send may include personal, confidential or sensitive information from your document. Apple processes these requests under its own privacy and security practices. We don't operate the PCC servers, and the text you send and the responses do not pass through our servers.

With PCC off, the assistant uses on-device passage matching. If PCC is unavailable, its quota is reached or a request fails, the app may fall back to local matching and shows which method it used. A request already sent may have been processed by Apple before a failure or fallback. Turning PCC off stops future requests but cannot recall one already sent.

Assistant questions and results normally stay in the open session. Copying a result writes it to the device clipboard. Saving a summary as PDF creates a file on your device and adds it to the Library.

6. Audio and video transcription

The "Audio or Video to Text" tool turns the speech in a recording or video you choose into text. It needs iOS speech recognition permission; it does not use the microphone. Recognition runs on your device by default. If the language you choose is not available on your device, the app tells you before the job starts and uses Apple's speech recognition servers only if you agree for that single job. Your consent is not saved and is asked again next time. In that case the audio of the recording is sent to Apple, does not pass through our servers, and the resulting file is labeled accordingly. On newer iOS versions, the first use of a language may download a language pack from Apple; your recording is not sent for that download.

While a job runs, a temporary copy of the file and short audio segments are kept on your device and deleted when the job finishes, fails or is cancelled.

7. Read aloud, Face ID and camera

Read aloud uses iOS on-device speech synthesis. If you turn on App lock, iOS performs the Face ID or Touch ID check; the app has no access to your biometric data. The camera is used only to scan documents, and the photo library only to import the images you pick and to save results.

8. Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob); you can watch a rewarded ad to earn an extra conversion. Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission is requested for personalized ads. If you decline, your advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared and non-personalized ads are requested; this may not stop all processing by the provider. AdMob may process your IP address, device information and ad interactions to serve and measure ads and to prevent abuse. See Google's Privacy Policy for details. Pro removes ads. We don't knowingly send document content to advertising services.

9. Crash reports

When the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends the crash log, device model, iOS version and app version so we can fix the problem. Firebase Analytics is turned off; we don't collect usage analytics. Document content is not knowingly included in crash reports.

If you use "Send Error Report", the app's on-device log file is attached to your email; you can see it before you send.

10. Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are made through Apple's App Store. Your card details never reach us. RevenueCat is used to record purchases and keep track of subscriptions. RevenueCat receives an anonymous user ID, the product, price, currency, country and transaction date.

11. Retention and deletion

Files on your device stay until you delete them or remove the app; iOS may clear temporary files on its own. Removing the app deletes its data on your device. Copies you exported, clipboard content and device backups may remain separately. You can delete the "Formly" folder in iCloud Drive from the Files app. Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep their own data under their own policies.

12. Your rights

Under laws such as the GDPR and Turkey's KVKK, you can ask whether your data is processed, request information, correction or deletion, object to processing and withdraw consent. Write to destek@formlyapps.com.

13. Children

FormlyConvert is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

14. Changes

If this policy changes, the current text is published on this page and the date at the top is updated. Material changes are communicated as required by law.
